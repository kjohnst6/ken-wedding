Ceremony on Prospect Mountain

Join us for the ceremony at the Prospect Mountain Summit Overlook Pavilion.

Ceremony Time: 2:00 PM

Please arrive 15–20 minutes early to allow time for the drive up the mountain, parking, and getting settled at the pavilion. There is a gate at the entrance of the mountain. Please tell the attendant you are here for the Johnston-Girard Wedding and will not be charged.

After the ceremony, we’ll all head down to Glens Falls for cocktail hour and the reception at Morgan & Co.
