Ceremony on Prospect Mountain

We’re honored to have you join us for the ceremony at the Prospect Mountain Summit Overlook Pavilion.

Ceremony Time: 3:00 PM

Please plan to arrive 15–20 minutes early to allow time for the drive up the mountain, parking, and getting settled at the pavilion.

The summit can be breezy and cooler than town—layers and comfortable shoes are a good idea.

After the ceremony, we’ll all head down to Glens Falls for cocktail hour and the reception at Morgan & Co.

The day

Date: Saturday, September 26

Time: Reception beginning with cocktail hour at 3:30 PM

Reception Location:
Morgan & Co. Restaurant
Glens Falls, New York

The celebration will have an outdoorsy, relaxed feel—think cozy, natural, and comfortable.

Weather in late September can be cool, especially in the evening. We recommend:

  • Layers or a light jacket
  • Comfortable shoes suitable for the wedding and dancing

Schedule

  • 3:30 PM — Cocktail hour begins
  • 5:00 PM — Dinner is served
  • 6:00–10:00 PM — Dancing and party
  • 10:00 PM – close — Drinks downtown for anyone who wants to keep the night going

Please arrive a little before 3:30 PM so you have time to park, settle in, and enjoy the start of the evening with us. Parking is available at Morgan & Co., with additional street parking and a lot across Maple Street.
